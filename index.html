Minseong Kweon

I'm a MS student in MnRI at University of Minnesota, Twin Cities. Before, I received my bachelor’s degree at Pusan National University under the supervision of Prof. Jinsun Park. During my undergraduate studies, I visited USTC to conduct research on 3D scene reconstruction under the supervision of Prof. Xuejin Chen. My research interests lie in Neural Rendering, Image Retrieval, Visual Localization, and SLAM.
