Minseong Kweon

I’m MS student in Robotics at University of Minnesota, Twin Cities. Currently, I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Lingjie Liu at the Penn Computer Graphics Lab. Before, I received my bachelor’s degree at Pusan National University under the supervision of Prof. Jinsun Park.

My research interests lie in 3D computer vision and machine learning for physically grounded geometric perception in robotic and intelligent systems.
